Privacy

What nautilai plugins read, save, and send. Last updated 2026-10-04.

Short version

  • Randall Noval maintains nautilai. The plugins run on your machine, inside your AI agent.
  • I run no server. The plugins have no analytics or telemetry. I receive none of your data.
  • Some plugins call services you already use, such as GitHub, with your own accounts. The table below lists each one.
  • You decide what the plugins do. You are responsible for the actions you approve.

For every plugin

  • Your AI provider processes the conversation, including any files, diffs, or command output that a plugin reads. The provider's privacy policy covers that data, for example Anthropic's.
  • When a plugin calls an outside service, that service's terms and privacy policy apply.
  • Plugins can read files in the project you open and in your agent's settings folder, such as ~/.claude/.

Health, privacy, and security tools

phi-scan, rbac-django, sentry-hygiene, and the security checks in other plugins are aids. They use pattern matching and AI review, and they can miss problems or flag things that are fine. They do not make you compliant with HIPAA, GDPR, or any other law or standard. I do not sign Business Associate Agreements. You remain responsible for your own compliance and for reviewing what these tools report.

Your responsibility and no warranty

The plugins act with your accounts and permissions. Review what they propose before you approve it. The plugins are provided "as is", without warranty of any kind, under the MIT License. To the extent the law allows, I am not liable for any loss or damage from using them.

Children

The plugins are developer tools. They are not made for people under 18.

Changes and contact

I update this page when a plugin's data handling changes. The date at the top shows the last change. The page history shows every change.

For a privacy or security concern, report it privately on GitHub. For other questions, open an issue. Issues are public, so do not include personal data.
